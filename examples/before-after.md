# Anti-AI Writing: before and after

The facts, names, and numbers in these samples are invented for illustration. The skill itself never invents numbers: when it lacks a specific, it asks you for one or leaves a `[bracket]` to fill.

## 1. Negative parallelism (the #1 tell)

**Before**

> It's not about the prompt. It's about the context.

**After**

> The model gets about 3x better when you paste the previous chat into the system prompt. `[swap in your own result]`

**What changed:** the rejected half was deleted, and the other half became a direct claim with a mechanism.

## 2. LinkedIn post opener

**Before**

> In today's fast-paced digital landscape, it's important to note that leveraging AI can unlock transformative growth for your business.

**After**

> I run a four-person agency. Last month AI cut our proposal time from 3 hours to 40 minutes. Here is exactly what I changed.

**What changed:** dead opening, three vocab tells, and a vague claim replaced with a lived detail.

## 3. Vague compression

**Before**

> Users were frustrated with the onboarding experience, which presented significant challenges.

**After**

> 42% of users quit on step 2, where the form asked for billing before showing the product.

## 4. Metaphor control

**Before**

> Your onboarding is a leaky bucket, and fixing it is the flywheel that will supercharge your growth.

**After**

> Users leave during onboarding. Fix step 2 first.

## 5. Engagement bait

**Before**

> This changes everything. Let that sink in. What's the best hire you ever made? 👇

**After**

> Our best hire was a part-timer we almost passed on. What did your best hire look like on paper?

**What changed:** the bait phrases are gone. The question is specific enough that someone can actually answer it.

## 6. Audit mode

**Input:** "They're not just employees. They're the DNA of everything we do."

```
ANTI-AI AUDIT:
  Negative parallelism: FLAG - "They're not just employees. They're the DNA of everything we do."
  Metaphor:             FLAG - "DNA of everything" (banned family) - say what they actually shape
  Specificity:          FLAG - no names, numbers, or lived detail
  Rewrite: "Maya in support rewrote our refund policy in April. Complaints dropped by half."
```

## 7. When to leave it alone

> I quit on a Tuesday. Told no one. Slept for 14 hours.

Specific, short, human. Nothing to fix.
